## 8. FAQ

**Q: Do my customers need to create an account or download an app to order from my store?**
A: No — and that's intentional. Your FreshCart store is a regular web page that opens in any smartphone or desktop browser. Customers tap your link, browse your fruits, add items to their cart, and confirm their order in under 60 seconds. No account creation, no app download, no friction.

**Q: I sell fruits by the kilo, not by the piece. Can FreshCart handle that?**
A: Yes. When you add a product you choose the unit of sale: per kilo, per unit, per box, per bunch, or any custom unit you define. Customers enter the quantity they want at checkout and the total is calculated automatically. You can also set minimum order quantities per product.

**Q: What happens when I run out of a fruit? Will customers still be able to order it?**
A: No. You can mark any product as 'Out of Stock' instantly from your phone, and it will show as unavailable in your store immediately. You can also set a stock quantity limit per product — when stock hits zero, the product is automatically hidden from your catalog so you never accept an order you can't fulfill.

**Q: How do I get paid? Does FreshCart take a percentage of my sales?**
A: FreshCart does not charge a transaction fee or take a cut of your sales — ever. You collect payment directly from your customers using the method you already use: cash on delivery, bank transfer, or a digital wallet like Nequi, Daviplata, or Mercado Pago. You display your payment instructions in your store settings and customers see them at checkout. Built-in payment gateway integration (where customers pay online at checkout) is on our roadmap and will be available for Pro plan users in Q3.

**Q: Can I manage my store from my phone while I'm at the market?**
A: Yes, completely. FreshCart is designed mobile-first. Your seller dashboard is fully functional in any smartphone browser — you can add or update products, adjust prices, check incoming orders, mark orders as fulfilled, and view your daily revenue all from your phone without needing a laptop.

**Q: I'm not technical at all. How hard is the setup?**
A: If you can write a WhatsApp message, you can set up FreshCart. Our onboarding wizard walks you through every step: enter your store name, upload a photo, add your first product, set your price, and hit publish. Most sellers complete setup in under 10 minutes. If you get stuck at any point, our support team is available by WhatsApp chat and email.

**Q: Can I use my own domain name instead of a FreshCart link?**
A: Custom domain support (e.g., frutas-maria.com pointing to your FreshCart store) is available on the Pro plan. On Starter and Growth plans your store URL will be yourname.freshcart.app, which is clean, professional, and easy to share. You can switch to a custom domain at any time by upgrading.

**Q: What if I want features that FreshCart doesn't have yet?**
A: We ship new features every two weeks based directly on what our sellers request. You can submit a feature request from inside your dashboard and vote on requests from other sellers. Our current roadmap includes built-in online payments, WhatsApp order notifications, delivery zone management, and a customer loyalty program. Pro plan sellers get early access to all new features before general release.
